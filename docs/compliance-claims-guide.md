# Cosmetic claims language (Australia)

Use this file for shop copy, hubs, ads, and AI drafts. It is an internal writing guide, not legal advice. TGA advertising rules for therapeutic goods and the ACL still apply. Have a qualified reviewer check high-risk pages (menopause, hair, post-procedure) before they go live.

## What we sell

- Leave-on **cosmetics** (serums, balms).
- **Cosmetic devices** and hygiene tools for an at-home protocol.
- Permitted **listed-style supplements** where we actually stock them.

We do **not** sell injectable research peptides, compounded peptide vials, grey-market GLP-1s, or anabolic oils/tablets.

## Claim language

Prefer appearance / cosmetic framing:

| Avoid (therapeutic / drug-style) | Prefer (cosmetic) |
| --- | --- |
| treats, cures, heals, repairs the skin as a medical act | supports the look of, helps improve the appearance of |
| eliminates wrinkles, anti-wrinkle medicine | the look of expression lines |
| treats hair loss, regrows hair, prevents baldness | the appearance of fuller-looking hair (only for a real topical scalp SKU, with a hub disclaimer) |
| treats menopause, HRT alternative, hormonal therapy | skin that can look drier or less firm around midlife (cosmetic framing + disclaimer) |
| clinical aftercare that implies a medical procedure we provide | after cosmetic needling, as part of a leave-on routine |

Do not imply ARTG registration, TGA evaluation, or medicine status for cosmetics. Do not put “TGA-approved” on every shop SKU unless that SKU is actually included on the ARTG for the advertised use. The shop shield badge must never appear on encyclopaedia or research-peptide pages.

## Required shop label

Every product card and future PDP must include the baked-in line:

**Not for injection — topical use only**

Do not leave this as optional per-SKU copy.

## Encyclopaedia vs shop

The peptide encyclopaedia and oils/tablets library are **educational**. They may describe unapproved or injectable compounds with “not sold” status. That content must not appear in the shop header or as a way to buy those compounds.

## Menopause and hair hubs (when built)

Add a visible disclaimer that the page is cosmetic information, not diagnosis or treatment of menopause, androgenetic alopecia, or any disease, and that customers should speak to a registered health practitioner for medical concerns.

## Experts and endorsements

Do not name a clinician, researcher, or public figure as an advisor, reviewer, or endorser unless they have a current written agreement. Public science can be cited with a reference; that is not the same as “Meet our expert.”
